    Approach

    Building software around real problems

    Technology is most useful when it solves an actual problem.

    My projects often begin with a workflow that is manual, repetitive, difficult to track, or dependent on disconnected tools. I approach development by understanding the workflow first, then designing the data, application logic, controls, and interface around it.

    The goal is not simply to add features, but to create systems that are understandable, maintainable, and useful.
